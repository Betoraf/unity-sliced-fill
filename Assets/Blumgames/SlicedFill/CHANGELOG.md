# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-15

- Fixed horizontal and vertical fills resizing the sprite instead of revealing it.
- Full-size slice positions and UVs now stay fixed for every Fill Amount and origin.
- Existing Image settings and prefab references are preserved when upgrading.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-15

- Sliced Fill checkbox directly in the standard Filled Image inspector.
- Horizontal, vertical and radial fill support with sprite borders.
- Automatic setup, Undo / Redo, multi-selection and prefab support.
- Standalone runtime/editor assemblies, sample prefab and English documentation.
